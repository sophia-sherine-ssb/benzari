The product

The wax that takes the heat.

Golden Fleece is a jacket of paraffin wax for pad-mounted transformers, plus a dashboard that shows which units are running hot. The wax absorbs heat passively, with no fans or pumps.

Schematic of the wax. Not measured data.

Melting is the trick.

Wax can soak up a large amount of heat while it changes from solid to liquid, and it stays at nearly the same temperature while it does. That flat stretch on the curve is the buffer. When the load drops, the wax gives the heat back to the evening air and solidifies.

Solid
Normal load. The wax sits at the transformer's temperature.
Melting
Heat arrives and the wax starts to melt. Its temperature holds.
Absorbing
The peak passes while the wax is still taking the heat in.
Resetting
Evening air cools the wax and it solidifies for the next day.
