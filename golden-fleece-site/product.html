The wax that takes the heat.

A jacket of paraffin wax for pad-mounted transformers, plus a dashboard that shows which units run hot.

DRAG TO ROTATE

Melting is the trick.

Wax soaks up a large amount of heat while it changes from solid to liquid, and stays at nearly the same temperature while it does. That flat stretch is the buffer. When the load drops, the wax gives the heat back to the evening air and solidifies.
