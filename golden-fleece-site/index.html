The problem

Transformers overheat when everyone needs power most.

In September 2024, an LA heat wave overheated the grid's equipment, and more than 64,000 LADWP customers lost power.

Sources: LAist reporting and LADWP public updates, September 2024.

How it works

A melting buffer for the worst hours.

01

Heat flows in

At peak load, heat moves from the transformer into the wax.

02

The wax melts

Melting stores a lot of heat while the wax stays at one temperature, so the peak stays lower.

03

It resets overnight

Evening air cools the wax and it solidifies, ready for the next day.

Result (simulated)

A peak about 25°C lower.
