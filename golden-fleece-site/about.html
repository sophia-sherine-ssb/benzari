About

We want the grid to stay cool.

Golden Fleece started as a student project at USC. In September 2024, an LA heat wave overheated grid equipment and left more than 64,000 LADWP customers without power. We wanted to know whether something simple could keep a transformer from getting there.

Why wax, and why now.

Transformers get hottest exactly when everyone turns on their air conditioning. Most cooling solutions depend on pumps or fans, and those can fail in the same heat wave. Paraffin wax needs neither. It melts, soaks up the heat, and gives it back overnight.

Heat waves are getting more frequent, and the equipment we rely on was built for milder weather. That is why we think a passive buffer is worth testing now.
