Engineering  ·  Design  ·  Biomedicine

From the deserts of Morocco to the palm trees of Los Angeles, I work at the intersection of design, technology, and economics. After receiving my French Baccalaureate from Lycée Descartes in Rabat, I went on to study Biomedical Engineering at USC as part of the Class of 2028.

From oil painting in my backyard to generating CAD from hand sketches, I've explored as many scientific and artistic tools as I could from a young age. Over time, I've grown to value the process of designing and building technology that can meaningfully shape the world around us.

This space brings together projects I've worked on, along with what I've learned through them. For more, see the /about section for my resume and contact information.
